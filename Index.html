Terms & Rules

Rule 1
Secrets stay here.
Whatever is shared between us stays between us. Don't tell anyone else. A secret shared is no longer a secret.
Rule 2
Bad mood? Tell me first.
If something bad happens, you're upset, or your mood is off, tell me first. Don't bottle it up or pretend everything is fine.
Rule 3
Talk directly.
If something is bothering you, ask me directly. Don't overthink. Don't assume things. Don't create ten different stories before hearing the real one.
Rule 4
No lying.
Never lie. If you can't tell me something, simply say: "I can't tell you right now." That's completely okay. A delayed truth is always better than a fake one.
Penalty
Penalty Clause
Anyone who knowingly breaks any of the above rules shall pay one month's salary to the other person. No excuses. No loopholes. No "that's not what I meant." The Friendship Court's decision is final.
